AUCTION LAYER BENCHMARK
=======================
Fabricated demo auction: 12 owners, 310-player pool, 3,000 seasons per arm.
Every input is generated by a formula; none of it describes a real player.

Timings are min and median of three repetitions (twenty or fifty for the
cheap operations), on an otherwise idle machine. An earlier capture taken
while a clean-checkout test run was competing for CPU came out roughly 35%
slower across the board, which is worth knowing before comparing numbers
from different sessions.

NOTHING HERE IS LIVE-CAPABLE. A single buy/pass comparison is seconds and a
reservation range is minutes. A 10-second bid timer would need a precomputed
table, not this search.

state validation (12 owners, 310 players)      min    0.001s  median    0.001s
legal maxima for all 12 owners                 min    0.000s  median    0.000s
fingerprint                                    min    0.000s  median    0.000s
completion search, proxy only                  min    0.428s  median    0.429s
completion search + CE (4 finalists x 3k seasons) min    3.891s  median    3.894s
buy/pass, unavailable                          min    2.552s  median    2.552s
buy/pass, named rival (rival re-completes)     min    3.538s  median    3.538s
reservation, 1 scenario x 6 prices             min   15.818s  median   15.818s

projected: full 54-scenario x 12-price reservation = 28 min
projected: 54-cell slot swap at 16k seasons (measured) = 23 min
