AUCTION LAYER BENCHMARK
=======================
Fabricated demo auction: 12 owners, 310-player pool, 3,000 seasons for the
selection sample and 3,000 for the independent holdout.
Every input is generated by a formula; none of it describes a real player.

Timings are min and median of three repetitions (twenty or fifty for the
cheap operations), on an otherwise idle machine.

WHAT THE AUDIT COST. Before the correction a buy/pass comparison took 2.55s,
because each branch stopped at its expected-points proxy and simulated one
roster. Evaluating the finalists by championship equity in BOTH branches,
optimising the rival's continuation for HIS OWN equity, and reporting the
result on an independent holdout sample takes 11.18s -- about 4.4x. The
rival path is 18.3s, about 5.2x. That is the price of computing the
quantity the documentation always claimed, and the earlier number should
not be quoted as though the two measured the same thing.

A full 54-scenario x 12-price reservation range is now projected at about
two hours rather than half an hour.

NOTHING HERE IS LIVE-CAPABLE. A single buy/pass comparison is eleven seconds
and a reservation range is over a minute per scenario. A 10-second bid timer
would need a precomputed table, not this search.
