### Where the swing actually comes from

Quantified, not hand-waved:

* **Roster construction, not candidate scoring.** Passing costs us *more*
  ($102 vs $89) and buys us *less*
  (101.68 vs 104.90 proxy points). Five players
  differ between the arms, not one. The candidate is not worth 3.2 proxy
  points on his own; losing him forces a worse and dearer completion.
* **Rival strength barely moves**: field mean 106.43 when
  they get him vs 106.16 when we do, a
  +0.27 shift across eleven teams.
  Denial value is real but small here.
* **Playoff-cutoff convexity amplifies it.** We sit last in the league in
  both arms, where a few proxy points move championship probability more
  than they would mid-table. This is why a ~3-point proxy gap becomes a
  ~0.03 equity gap.
* **Allocation instability contributes** and is not separable at this
  sample size: the non-monotone $1/$5/$10 rows show the finalist set
  shifting with the shadow clearing.
